Blazing Trails Tour

Community & Free Initiatives

We believe cannabis education should be accessible to all. Through our Blazing Trails Tour series, we host free, community-centered nature walks across Kansas City.

Even with our paid flagship tours temporarily paused, our commitment to Kansas City remains stronger than ever. Blazing Trails Tour is our signature community outreach program, organizing free 21+ cannabis-friendly community trail walks — completely free to the public, fostering local pride, outdoor wellness, and accessible science-based cannabis education.
