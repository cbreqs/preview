Our Story

Elevating Kansas City

Organized on September 3, 2025, Elevated Adventures is Kansas City's premier cannabis education tour company, operating a dual B2B2C model.

Founded on Education, Community, & Innovation

Elevated Adventures, LLC was organized on September 3, 2025, with a clear mission: to provide high-end, expert-led cannabis education that empowers consumers while building an authentic, compliant marketing platform for local brands.

Under the leadership of Lady Verletta Martin, Founder & CEO, the company was created to address a critical gap in the market. While cannabis legalization has opened up incredible opportunities, advertising restrictions make it nearly impossible for quality brands to connect with curious consumers. Elevated Adventures bridges this gap through immersive, curated tours.

We pair cannabis science with Kansas City's award-winning culinary scene, artisan craft breweries, and rich local history, offering a premium experience that is safe, structured, and completely compliant with state laws.

Lady Verletta Martin

Founder & CEO

Lady Verletta is a passionate community builder and entrepreneur. Her vision for Elevated Adventures was born from a desire to demystify the cannabis plant, highlight local KC culinary favorites, and support the growth of the regional craft business ecosystem.
