B2B Frequently Asked Questions

How do you verify compliance and age?

We employ strict, manual 21+ photo ID checks before any guest participates in our events, boards our tour vehicles, or enters private partner spaces.

Can we sell products during the tours?

Elevated Adventures is an educational and experiential marketing platform. We do not distribute or sell cannabis products — our tour stops drive guests directly into your licensed retail dispensaries or boutique hemp shops for compliant purchases.

What is the sponsorship timeline?

Blazing Trails sponsorships are booked on a rolling basis. Official tour stops on our flagship paid itineraries are booked on a seasonal or quarterly contract basis.

How do we book Private Group Tours?

Select "Private Group Tour Inquiry" in the form above, or call us directly at 816-433-1232.
