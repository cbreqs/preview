Our Story

The Cannabis Network of Kansas City started as an online spark and has grown into a vibrant community of cannabis lovers, advocates, and friends. Here, we connect, learn, and grow together, sharing conversations, classes, and opportunities that strengthen our bonds and celebrate the plant that brings us together.

Our Mission

Community driven

Education

We know our patrons want opportunities to learn so we want to aid in providing those opportunities. We provide a variety of classes for our members to choose from.

Community

When we say 'local' we mean it. It is our goal to build up and support the surrounding cannabis community through regular charity events and community service.

Connection

Cannabis has always brought people together! Host your cannabis events with us and give your guests the elevated experience of a lifetime.
