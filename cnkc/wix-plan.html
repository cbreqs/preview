Making the site easier to update

You mentioned that putting up a new event takes way more work than it should. I went through the site to see why, and there's a fix that doesn't mean leaving Wix or learning anything new.

Why it takes so long right now

When the Events page was built, each event got typed into its own text box and dragged into place by hand. Wix lets you work that way, and for a page you set up once it's fine. The catch is that nothing on the page knows it's looking at an event. It's just a stack of boxes sitting in positions you chose. So every time you add one, you're doing the layout over again from scratch.

That's also why things drift out of order, and why old events sit there until somebody remembers to take them down. Here's what's actually on each page:

/events Rebuild

9 hand-placed text boxes · 4 loose images · 0 templates

Nine separate boxes for four events. The dates are in three different formats right now, "Wednesday, July 15th", "8/3/26", and "Monday July 27th 2026", and they're not in date order, because nothing is sorting them.

/hubs — Business Members Rebuild

18 hand-placed text boxes · 7 loose images · 0 templates

Five members, eighteen boxes. Every logo, blurb, category and link gets placed on its own, so adding a member is the same slow job as adding an event.

/gallery Already good

25 photos · 1 gallery element

This one's already set up the right way. All 25 photos are in a single Wix gallery, so adding more is a few clicks with no dragging involved. If photos have been feeling like a hassle, I'd guess it's because event pictures were going onto the Events page instead of here.

Two things that are quietly out of date

The home page still lists the Harvesters Food Bank volunteer day on November 22nd, 2025, so it's about nine months back now. Easy to miss, since it's not the page you'd think to go update. Once events live in one place the home page can pull from it and stay current on its own.

The Business Members page is still titled "Employee Retreats". That title doesn't show up on the page itself, it's the browser tab and the headline Google shows in search results, so anyone searching for your small business members is seeing something unrelated. It's a quick change in that page's settings, and worth doing because it's costing you the search traffic that page should be getting.

What I'd change

Two pages get rebuilt using tools that come with the plan you're already on. The look stays the same, black background, white type, same fonts, so nobody visiting will notice a difference. The difference is on your end.

Events → the Wix Events app

Wix has a tool built specifically for this and it's free to install. It handles everything the text boxes were doing by hand.

  • Events get added from your dashboard instead of the editor, so you're not opening the drag-and-drop canvas to post one anymore.
  • They sort themselves by date, so the ordering problem goes away.
  • You can set the list to show upcoming only, and once a date passes it comes down on its own. That's one less thing to keep track of.
  • Free RSVPs come with it, so you'd get a rough headcount for Girl Bluntz or SMOGA ahead of time if you want one.
  • Something like the twice-monthly coffee networking gets set up once as a repeating event.

If you ever want to sell tickets, that's possible later. It needs a Business Premium plan and Wix takes 2.5%. Free events and RSVPs don't cost anything.

Business Members → a CMS collection

There's no off-the-shelf app for this one, so I'd build a small table instead. You fill in a row for each member and the page draws them all the same way.

Adding an event, start to finish

Once this is set up, here's the whole thing. It all happens in your dashboard, so the editor stays closed.

  1. Go to manage.wix.com and open your site.

  2. Click Events in the left menu.

  3. Click + Create Event.

  4. Put in the name, date, time and location, and add a photo if you have one.

    For location you can use a real address like "Cafe Ca Phe, 916 E 5th St" and it'll show a map.

  5. Write the description, same kind of thing you're typing now.

  6. Turn on RSVP if you want a headcount, or leave it off for the no-ticket events.

  7. Click Publish. It shows up on the Events page right away, in the right order.

    No layout involved, and when the date passes it takes itself down.

Adding photos

This part doesn't change, it already works. I'm writing it down because it's easy to not realize how quick it is.

  1. Open the Gallery page in the editor.

  2. Click the gallery, then Manage Media.

  3. Click Add Media and upload from your computer or your phone. Drag any photo to reorder it.

    Event photos should go here rather than on the Events page. That's most likely what's been making photos feel like work.

  4. Hit Publish in the top right.

Adding a business member

  1. In your dashboard, open CMS. It might be labeled Content Manager.

  2. Open the Business Members collection.

  3. Click + New Item.

  4. Fill in the seven fields, name through display order.

  5. Close it and the page updates itself.

    To take someone down, delete the row or untick its visibility. Nothing to remove from the page.

What I'd be doing

The setup work on my end, roughly a day.

  • Install Wix Events & Tickets.
  • Re-enter the current events as real events: Coffee & Networking, Higher Ladies Club, Sound Mandala, Summer Kick-Off.
  • Set Coffee & Networking to repeat twice a month.
  • Style the event list to match the site, black ground, white type, same headings.
  • Set the list to upcoming only.
  • Clear the 9 old text boxes and 4 loose images off /events.
  • Build the Business Members collection with the seven fields.
  • Move the five current members into it, logos and all.
  • Set up one repeater on /hubs, connect it to the collection, sort by displayOrder.
  • Clear the 18 old text boxes and 7 loose images off /hubs.
  • Fix the /hubs page title that still says "Employee Retreats".
  • Point the home page events blurb at the live list so it can't go stale again.
  • Sit down with you and add one real event together, start to finish.

What I'd leave alone

About, Memberships and Contact stay as they are. They're built by hand too, but they hardly ever change, and hand-placed is fine for a page you're not constantly editing. Redoing them would be a bunch of work you'd never see the benefit of.

The drag-and-drop editor is still there for everything. If you want to nudge a heading or swap out a background, that works exactly like it does today.
